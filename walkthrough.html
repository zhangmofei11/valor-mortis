Complete Walkthrough

Chapter-by-chapter guide with detailed objectives and secret locations.

I

Chapter 1: The Conscript

Training Camp → First Battle

Main Story
Duration:1-2 hours
Collectibles:5/12
Difficulty:Easy

Objectives:

  • • Complete basic combat training
  • • Learn movement and dodge mechanics
  • • Defeat the Training Instructor (mini-boss)
  • • Equip your first proper weapon
II

Chapter 2: The March

Military Camp → Austrian Border

Main Story
Duration:2-3 hours
Collectibles:8/15
Difficulty:Normal

Objectives:

  • • March to the Austrian border
  • • Learn parry and stance switching
  • • Encounter first real enemies
  • • Find the hidden Musketeer uniform
III

Chapter 3: The Battle of Ulm

Ulm Fortress → City Liberation

Main Story
Duration:3-4 hours
Collectibles:12/20
Difficulty:Hard

Objectives:

  • • Infiltrate Ulm Fortress
  • • Disable the artillery positions
  • • Defeat Commander von Auston (Boss)
  • • Liberate the city
IV

Chapter 4: The Pursuit

Alpine Pass → Italian Border

Side Quest
Duration:2-3 hours
Collectibles:6/18
Difficulty:Normal

Objectives:

  • • Track the Austrian spy through the Alps
  • • Survive the mountain ambush
  • • Optional: Save the stranded villagers
V

Chapter 5: Austerlitz

The Decisive Battle

Critical
Duration:4-5 hours
Collectibles:10/25
Difficulty:Very Hard

Objectives:

  • • Survive the opening bombardment
  • • Capture the Pratzen Heights
  • • Defeat Marshal Ney (Mini-boss)
  • • Face the Emperor's Guard (Final Boss)
