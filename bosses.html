Boss Guide

Comprehensive strategies for defeating all major bosses in Valor Mortis.

Commander von Auston

Chapter 3: The Battle of Ulm

HP 2,400
Difficulty Hard

Attack Patterns

Sword Slash Combo Aggressive

3-hit combo. Dodge sideways on 2nd hit.

Shield Bash Stun

Charges forward with shield. Parry or back dodge.

Call Reinforcements Phase 2

Summons 2 soldiers. Focus von Auston, ignore adds.

Strategy

1

Stay in Middle Guard throughout the fight for balanced defense.

2

Wait for his sword combo, then punish with LML during recovery frames.

3

At 50% HP he calls reinforcements. Use this time to deal heavy damage.

4

Final phase: He becomes aggressive. Use Low Guard parry for counter attacks.

Recommended Level: 12-14 | Recommended Weapon: Heavy saber with high stagger

Marshal Ney

Chapter 5: Austerlitz

HP 3,800
Difficulty Very Hard

Attack Patterns

Cavalry Charge Dangerous

Mounted charge across arena. Dodge perpendicular to charge direction.

Saber Storm AoE

Rapid 8-hit spin attack. Run away until opening.

War Cry Buff

Damage boost for 10s. Interrupt with charged attack if possible.

Strategy

1

Phase 1: Stay at medium range. Punish his cavalry dismount animation.

2

When he mounts, circle around arena edge to avoid charge.

3

Use High Guard for aggression when he's dismounted and vulnerable.

4

Interrupt War Cry with your own ultimate ability for free damage.

Recommended Level: 22-24 | Recommended Weapon: Bayonet with high reach

The Emperor's Guard

Chapter 5: Final Boss

HP 6,000
Difficulty Extreme

Attack Patterns

Imperial Decree Phase 1

Area denial attack. Multiple shockwave zones appear. Find the safe gap.

Guard Formation Adds

Summons 4 elite guards in formation. Target guards to break formation.

Emperor's Wrath Final Phase

Unleashes all previous attacks in rapid succession. Survive for 20 seconds.

Strategy

1

Use environment cover during Imperial Decree to avoid shockwaves.

2

Guard Formation: Kill guards quickly with AoE attacks to prevent overwhelm.

3

Final phase: Low Guard parry everything. Counter attack when safe.

4

Save ultimate for Emperor's Wrath phase for maximum burst damage.

Recommended Level: 28-30 | Recommended Weapon: Balanced saber with healing proc
