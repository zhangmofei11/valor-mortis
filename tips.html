Tips & Tricks

Maximize your gameplay with essential tips, hidden secrets, and XP farming methods.

B Beginner Tips

1

Master the Parry First

Perfect parries are the most important skill in Valor Mortis. Spend time in Training Camp practicing timing before progressing.

2

Explore Every Area

Hidden chests and documents are everywhere. Check walls, behind crates, and any unusual shadows.

3

Upgrade Stamina First

More stamina means more dodges and blocks. Prioritize stamina upgrades over damage early game.

4

Read All Documents

Story documents unlock background lore and sometimes reveal secret locations or treasure.

$ XP Farming Methods

Alpine Pass Run

2.5k XP/run

After completing Chapter 4, return to Alpine Pass. Clear enemies in 3 minutes, restart checkpoint. Best early-game farm.

Time: 3 min Chapter 4+

Ulm Fortress Gauntlet

5k XP/run

Speedrun through Ulm Fortress ignoring main objectives. Kill all soldiers and commanders. Respawn at checkpoint.

Time: 8 min Chapter 3+

Austerlitz Cavalry Camp

8k XP/run

In Chapter 5, after Pratzen Heights capture, clear cavalry camp before advancing. High enemy density.

Time: 5 min Chapter 5

Boss Rush Mode

15k XP/run

Post-game only. Challenge Mode lets you fight all bosses consecutively with bonus XP multiplier.

Time: 45 min Post-game

? Hidden Secrets

Musketeer Disguise

In Chapter 2, find the hidden uniform in the farmhouse basement. Walk past Austrian checkpoints undetected.

Hidden Cache

Shoot the wooden barrel near Ulm fortress gate. It explodes revealing a secret passage with rare weapon.

Secret Date

Complete all side quests before Chapter 4. Isabelle will invite you to dinner - affects ending sequence.

A Advanced Strategies

Stamina Management

Never let stamina hit zero. At 0 stamina, you cannot dodge or block for 2 seconds. Always keep 20% minimum.

Crowd Control

Use environment to your advantage. Lure multiple enemies to narrow passages where they can't surround you.

Time Your Attacks

Watch enemy attack animations. Most have 0.5s windup. Start your attack during their windup to guarantee hit.

Perfect Parry Window

The perfect parry window is 200ms before impact. Audio cue: listen for enemy weapon whoosh, then press block.
