Combat Guide

Master the art of Napoleonic warfare with our comprehensive combat system guide.

1 Combat Stances

High Guard

Aggressive stance with increased attack power. Best for pressure tactics and staggered enemies.

Difficulty: Low

Middle Guard

Balanced stance for offense and defense. Ideal for beginners and general combat.

Difficulty: Easy

Low Guard

Defensive stance with superior parry window. Perfect for counters and punish tactics.

Difficulty: High

2 Core Mechanics

Parry System

1
Perfect Parry

Press block 200ms before impact. Reflects damage and staggers enemy.

2
Normal Block

Hold block to reduce damage by 60%. Stamina cost varies by weapon.

3
Counter Attack

After successful parry, press heavy attack for 2x damage counter.

Stance Switching

Q
Quick Switch

Instant stance change. Maintains momentum but no defensive bonus.

E
Power Switch

0.5s delay but grants 15% damage boost for 3 seconds.

R
Ultimate Switch

Fully charged switch unlocks special stance ability.

3 Basic Combos

LML → Quick Slash Combo

3-hit combo with increasing damage. Ends with slight knockback.

LMH → Sweeping Strike

Light into heavy. Final hit knocks down grounded enemies.

HHL → Armor Pierce

Heavy attack chain that ignores 40% of enemy armor.

L+R → Stance Burst

Dual input unleashes weapon-specific special attack.

4 Advanced Techniques

Bayonet Charge

Sprint + Heavy Attack. Covers distance quickly, impales enemies on contact for bleed damage over 5 seconds.

Stamina: High

Flourish Parry

Perfect parry during enemy attack chain. Slows time for 1.5s allowing critical hit setup.

Timing: Precise

Musket Ignition

After bayonet strike, ignite enemy with flintlock spark. Explosive damage in 3m radius.

Cooldown: 8s

Formation Break

Target enemy group leader. Defeating them causes remaining enemies to lose coordination and attack slower.
